## Fee structure and scholarships for 2026–2027 (from supplied images)

The following is a transcription of the five supplied fee images. The brochure title says **Fees 2026–2027**. Amounts are reproduced as printed and shown in rupees (₹). The course-fee pages do not label the amounts individually as annual, but the University’s [2026–27 Student Handbook fee policy](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/69c27ebd7d39a1774354109.pdf) says University fees are charged annually in advance and may be paid in two instalments. Treat these figures as annual amounts consistent with that policy, and confirm the programme-specific amount with Admissions before quoting it. The scholarship page explicitly uses fee-per-year thresholds. Course-name spelling is lightly normalized where the image OCR was unclear.

Source images in the workspace: `fee 2.jpeg` (cover and p. 2), `fee3.jpeg` (pp. 3–4), `fee5.jpeg` (pp. 5–6), `fee 4.jpeg` (pp. 7–8), and `fee structure.jpeg` (pp. 8–9; page 8 repeats). The figures below were read from these images; no fee values were inferred from other sources.

### Cross-check against University PDFs and admissions pages

I checked the workspace documents and the official 2026–27 University material available online. No separate course-wise fee table matching the supplied images was present in the workspace PDF set or in the 2026–27 school brochures reviewed. The brochures list programmes and admissions information, but do not independently confirm these price figures. The official [admissions enrolment page](https://presidencyuniversity.in/admissions/enrollment-process) directs applicants to programme advisors for programme-wise fees and scholarships.

The 2026–27 [Student Handbook](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/69c27ebd7d39a1774354109.pdf), section 17.1, confirms annual fee charging and two instalments; it gives fee policy, not course-by-course amounts. Official programme brochures reviewed for name/context checks include [Computer Science and Engineering](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/69490862c75421766393954.pdf), [MBA](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/694901e5d195e1766392293.pdf), [BBA](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/694902e166bd81766392545.pdf), [Information Science](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/6948fb1f622c01766390559.pdf), [Law](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/69492a304ec191766402608.pdf), and [Allied Health Sciences](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/694905d1da1b61766393297.pdf). These verify programme/school context only, not the amounts transcribed below.

**Unresolved amount check:** MBA Business Analytics is printed twice in the supplied images: ₹400,000 under MBA specialisations and ₹375,000 as “MBA (Business Analytics)”. Both entries are preserved as printed; the public brochures do not resolve whether these are distinct fee categories. Confirm with Admissions.
