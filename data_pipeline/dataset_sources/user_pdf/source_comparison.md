# Focus guide source audit

**Audit date:** 29 October 2026  
**Scope:** Checked the claims in `D:\pu_bot\dataset\FOCUS_GUIDE.md` against the University’s posted 2025 Academic Regulations, the current fees/financial-assistance/scholarship/Launchpad pages, the 2026–27 Student Handbook, and the uploaded CSE 2023–27 regulations. The guide itself and the main dataset were not changed.

## Overall finding

The guide is substantially supported. The 2025 Academic Regulations PDF is the University’s latest version shown on its Academic Regulations page, and it expressly covers existing degree programs. The most important caveat is cohort transition: it says effects of amendments on earlier entrants are to be handled carefully, and a student rejoining follows the regulations and PRC applicable to that student’s batch. For a disputed student-specific case, do not resolve the policy conflict from document dates alone; get the University’s interpretation for that batch.

## Cohort/program comparison: uploaded CSE 2023–27 PDF

The uploaded [B.Tech CSE Programme Regulations & Curriculum 2023–27](680f36b2a7f201745827506.pdf) agrees with the 2025 University rules on the basic 30% End-Term / 40% aggregate theory threshold and 40% lab/project aggregate threshold (§12.6.1–12.6.2, PDF pp. 12–13). It also provides a CSE-specific weighting table and a 160-credit CSE structure with a 4.50 minimum CGPA for degree eligibility (§§12.5, 14–16, PDF pp. 12–15). The current University rules likewise state a 4.50 minimum for UG degree awards (§19.2.2).

There is a material make-up-policy difference in what the documents expressly say: the CSE 2023–27 PDF describes F-grade make-up for theory courses and does not mention using make-up for D grades; the 2025 Academic Regulations expressly allow D-grade make-up to improve CGPA and specify a two-grade cap (§13.3.2). The older CSE document’s silence is not proof that D make-up was forbidden for that batch. Because §1.5 of the 2025 regulation addresses earlier-batch changes and the current CSE program page lists separate 2023–27, 2024–28, 2025–29, and 2026–30 PRCs, answers must use the student’s batch-specific PRC and confirm transition treatment when the two sources matter. [Current B.Tech CSE program page](https://presidencyuniversity.in/program/b-tech-computer-science-engineering).

The CSE PDF contains a course catalog only for the 2023–27 cohort. It is not a current course list for 2024–28 through 2026–30. The 2025 Academic Regulations say each program’s PRC contains program-specific eligibility, degree credits, assessment rules, course structure, prerequisites, and catalog (§§6.0–6.4). The guide’s advice to retrieve the student’s current PRC is correct.

## Fees, scholarship, and financial assistance

- **Program fee amounts:** The Enrolment Process page sends applicants to program advisors for program-wise fees and scholarships; it does not show current numeric tuition amounts. The official [2026–27 Student Handbook](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/69cbb0366476b1774956598.pdf) adds that University fees are annual (not semester-based), normally payable in two instalments (July/August and December/January), and that new-admission payment is specified in the individual provisional admission letter (printed pp. 63–64, §17.1). It gives no current program-wise tuition table. Therefore the guide is right to withhold numeric program fees until a current program/year-specific fee annexure or offer letter is available. [Enrolment Process](https://presidencyuniversity.in/admissions/enrollment-process), “Fee Structure & Scholarships”; [2026–27 Student Handbook](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/69cbb0366476b1774956598.pdf), §17.1.
- **Financial assistance:** The live page describes GrayQuest as a third-party, limited-period monthly EMI/loan option for annual tuition or hostel fees. It says the loan transaction is between the lender and student and creates no University obligation in respect of the loan. This is financing, not a University grant or guaranteed aid. The guide’s description is supported; the offer and terms should be rechecked before giving current advice. [Financial Assistance](https://presidencyuniversity.in/admissions/financial-assistance).
- **Scholarships:** The live page lists merit, sports, cultural, leadership/special achievement, Talent Quest Scholarship 2026, and AICTE schemes. It says awards are subject to eligibility and availability, normally only one may be awarded at a time unless specified, supporting documents are required, and the Scholarship Committee’s decision is final. The page does not publish amounts or full eligibility thresholds. Do not state a scholarship amount, entitlement, or current application deadline from these pages alone. [Scholarships](https://presidencyuniversity.in/admissions/scholarships).
- **Scholarship limits and timing:** The Student Handbook says scholarship categories/details and conditions are notified at admission for a new academic year; scholarships are tuition-fee rebates, generally for the first year of the relevant program, and students awarded a first-year scholarship pay full University fees and charges from the second year (printed pp. 45–46). This is a material condition missing from the guide summary; cite the cycle-specific scholarship policy if an exception may apply. [2026–27 Student Handbook](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/69cbb0366476b1774956598.pdf), pp. 45–46.

## Academic support and Launchpad

- **Mentoring:** Verified. Under §3.6 of the 2025 Academic Regulations, each incoming student is assigned a mentor to counsel and guide them on academic and registration matters; the student consults the mentor before registering courses. This supports the guide’s mentor statement, but does not by itself establish a specific tutoring/remedial service. [2025 Academic Regulations PDF](https://presidencyuniversity.in/uploads/images/68903c797f1721754283129.pdf), p. 8, §3.6.
- **Launchpad entity and facilities:** The university’s Innovation & Incubation page identifies Presidency LaunchPad Association as a Section 8 company incubator registered on 2 February 2022 with the University and Presidency Group; it says the existing infrastructure and equipped laboratories will be made available to incubated startups. Its page also describes an Innovative Projects course for freshmen. [Innovation & Incubation](https://presidencyuniversity.in/life-at-presidency/innovation-incubation).
- **Launchpad services:** The PLA page describes expert mentorship/advisory, investment support, pitch opportunities, seed/angel funding facilitation, grant/government-scheme support, and industry ecosystem connections. These are advertised services and pathways, not a promise that an applicant will receive funding. The “investor/grant connections,” “industry partnerships,” and “labs/facilities” claims in the guide are supported when read alongside the Innovation & Incubation page; Section 8 status is stated on that overview page, not on the PLA landing page itself. [Presidency LaunchPad Association](https://presidencyuniversity.in/innovation-incubation/presidency-launchpad-association) · [Innovation & Incubation](https://presidencyuniversity.in/life-at-presidency/innovation-incubation).

## Items to treat as unresolved in the RAG bot

1. Do not return a numeric tuition amount unless the bot retrieves a current program/year fee annexure or offer letter. The public Enrolment Process page directs students to an advisor, and the current Student Handbook confirms amounts are admission-letter-specific.
2. Do not tell a student the 2023–27 CSE make-up rule automatically overrides or is automatically overridden by the 2025 common rule. The common regulation is the current published framework, but it explicitly discusses careful treatment of earlier cohorts and rejoining against the relevant batch PRC. Ask for batch/program and cite both clauses when relevant.
3. The scholarship page has categories but not full award values/thresholds. A current cycle’s notification or written Scholarship Committee/admissions guidance is needed for those.
4. The website advertises Launchpad funding facilitation and reports activity metrics, but no eligibility or individual funding guarantee is established by these pages.
